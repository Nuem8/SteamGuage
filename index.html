SteamGauge

From the NueM8 works: a round brass dial, riveted and glassed. The needle wakes at rest, then sweeps the arc as the boilers take up pressure. Red bids you LOOK. Yellow bids you CHECK. Green says the engine is OK. One plate keeps its counsel behind a keyhole.

steamGauge.js draws a round steampunk gauge as SVG. An arc covers 80% of the circle, red through yellow to green, with the open gap at the bottom. The needle pivots at the center. Its point meets the arc, and a counterweight extends the opposite way. The label sits halfway from the hub to the arc. The readout sits in the bottom gap.

Pass a value from 0 to 1, or a percent above 1. On first draw the needle starts at zero and sweeps to that value over one second. Change data-value or call setSteamGauge and it sweeps from the current position to the new number, again over one second. The readout color follows the zone: red, yellow, then green.

Set data-readout="warning" to show LOOK, CHECK, or OK in place of the digits. The number still places the needle. Below 34 is LOOK, 34 to 66 is CHECK, and 67 and above is OK. Set data-readout="keyhole" to draw a lock keyhole in that same gap. Its rim uses the same red, yellow, and green. A gauge is any element with class steam-gauge, plus data-label, data-value, and data-size.

This page loads four gauges. Every 3.5 seconds one of them moves to a new number so the sweep, the warning word, and the keyhole can be seen.
